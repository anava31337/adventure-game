# Adventure Game
![adventure-game-image](https://www.dropbox.com/s/8gaqs5lsl30318s/Screen%20Shot%202023-05-22%20at%209.11.10%20PM%20%40%20300ppi.png?raw=1)

'Adventure Game' is a 2D side-scrolling fantasy/sci-fi action-adventure platformer RPG, similar in gameplay to Zelda II: The Adventure of Link, but with spiritual inspirations drawn from Super Mario World, Metroid and Elden Ring, with a focus on exploration, action-adventure elements and fast paced, timing-based combat. 

Explore a vibrant fantasy world with an ancient, buried and technologically advanced past. Play as the guard of a peaceful village who stumbles upon an existential threat taking form in this esoteric world. Traverse several large overworld map areas with lots of nooks and crannies, many rich side-scrolling levels full of secrets and lively towns with interesting NPCs. Find weapons, abilities and power-ups throughout your journey, face off against challenging enemies and bosses, use your wits and tools to thwart danger and survive harsh environments, and uncover the secrets and lore of this mysterious and ancient world.

'Adventure Game' is currently being developed in Unity 2022.3.52f1, and has no release date yet, though you can play the game in its somewhat current state [here](https://anava31337.itch.io/adventure-game). Other tools we use to manage the project include Visual Studio Code, GitHub, Tiled, Discord, Aseprite and Photoshop. Check out the Wiki/Project documentation [here](https://github.com/anava31337/adventure-game/wiki) for more detailed information. Stay tuned for more info, many upcoming improvements and new features coming soon!
